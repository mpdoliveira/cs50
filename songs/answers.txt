QUESTION 1:
Using the following SQL command:
SELECT AVG(energy), AVG(valence), AVG(danceability) FROM songs;
We get the averages 0.65906, 0.484443 and 0.71646 for energy, valence and danceability respectively.

Let's analyse this data:
- 0.65906 of energy means this person enjoys songs that are generally more energetic.
- 0.484443 of valence represents songs that are slightly more "negative", such as sad or angry songs.
- 0.71646 of danceability indicates that this person likes songs suitable for dancing.

Based on the info gathered, I'd say this person has an "Audio Aura" that is:
- VIBRANT, to indicate the energy.
- INTROSPECTIVE, to indicate a neutral shy-ish character.
- RHYTHMIC, to indicate the dance-oriented songs.
